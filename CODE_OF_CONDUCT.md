# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone who
participates in Uldren Core, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal or political attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or other collaboration.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including the repository, issues, pull
requests, and any communication conducted on behalf of the project in public
spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to coc@uldren.ai. Reports are handled
confidentially and reviewed promptly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate it, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they judge
harmful.
